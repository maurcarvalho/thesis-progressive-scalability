Before all else, I give thanks to the Holy God, for the grace of living in times prosperous enough to grant the labor of the mind its hours, and for the time, the attention, and the intelligence that could be offered to this work and called, in their measure, a small answer. From Tommaso d'Aquino and San Josemaría I learned that study and work, in whatever form they take, are vocations of holiness: that every hour given to reading, every problem held in steady thought, every effort of the intellectual life serves to sanctify the soul and to cultivate the human virtues by which alone, even in the smallest measure, the pages here presented may be carried to their end.\footnote{From the prayer \emph{Ante Studium} attributed to Tommaso d'Aquino: \emph{``Ingressum instruas, progressum dirigas, egressum compleas''}, instruct the beginning, direct the progress, complete the end.}

To my wife, whose active partnership saw this work through every stage, I owe more than these lines can hold. The encouragement you offered when the days were long, the conviction with which you held its purpose, and the goals you championed alongside mine are written into every page that follows. Without your generosity and your unfaltering faith, none of this would have had a foundation.

To the Instituto Tecnológico de Aeronáutica, and most especially to the professors who give their days to elevating the standing of Engineering in our country, I record my gratitude. It is a rare privilege to study within a tradition that so many notable engineers helped to build, and that so many continue to sustain still.

To my professional colleagues, whose practice posed the questions and whose conversations refined the answers, my thanks. What I learned from you in the daily craft is no less than what I learned from books and from the papers of this discipline, and this research drew from each with equal regard.

Last, and not least, to my advisors, Prof. Karla Donato Fook, Ph.D., and Prof. Juliana de Melo Bezerra, Ph.D., I am grateful for the patience that was never withheld, the direction that was always precise, the friendship forged along the way, and the generous collaboration at each stage of this research. Your attentive presence has been essential for the completion of this work.
